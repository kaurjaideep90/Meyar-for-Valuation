Who We Are

Professional expertise behind every valuation

Meyar for Valuation is a specialised valuation and appraisal firm, established in Muscat in 2025.

We deliver independent, well-documented opinions of value that help owners, investors and financing institutions make clearer, more precise and better-governed decisions. Every assignment is grounded in sound methodology, reliable data and a deep understanding of the local market.

Valuation Methodology

  1. 01

    Define the Purpose

  2. 02
